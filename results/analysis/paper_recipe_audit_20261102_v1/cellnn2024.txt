ARTICLE               INFO                              ABSTRACT

Dataset link: https://github.com/UESTC-nnLab            In texture classification, local binary pattern (LBP) is currently one of the most widely-concerned feature
/LBP_Contour                                            encoding models. Most existing LBP-based texture classification methods are usually limited to single-kind
                                                        texture features. In fact, an across-domain fusion of LBP features with other features, such as image contours,
Keywords:
Texture classification                                  could be another potential path to promote texture classification. To enhance the feature modeling ability of
Local binary pattern                                    LBP-based methods, this paper firstly designs a Cellular Neural Network (CellNN) with recurrent convolutions,
Cellular neural network                                 initially trained by a simplified simulated-annealing algorithm, to extract informative image contours. For
Image contour extraction                                better reliability, a new three-channel contour extractor of deep CellNNs (𝑖.𝑒., dCellNNs) is proposed. This
Multi-scale across-domain feature fusion                extractor contains the initially-trained CellNNs of more than three layers, and it is further optimized by fine-
                                                        tuning parameters. Moreover, a new weighted-base algorithm is designed to fulfill the fusion of the multi-scale
                                                        texture features by LBPs and the contour features by dCellNNs to enhance feature representation. Finally, these
                                                        enhanced features are concatenated together to generate the final multi-scale features of given texture image.
                                                        On texture datasets KTH, Brodatz, OTC12 and UIUC, experiment results verify that the across-domain fusion
                                                        of multi-scale LBPs and dCellNNs is efficient in capturing & enhancing texture features. With moderate feature
                                                        dimensionality and computational costs, it could improve texture classification, acquiring an obvious accuracy
                                                        increase on previous state-of-the-art ones, e.g., a rise of 2.58% on KTH-TIPS2b, a rise of 3.11% on Brodatz, a
                                                        rise of 0.71% on OTC12 and a rise of 0.42% on UIUC.
