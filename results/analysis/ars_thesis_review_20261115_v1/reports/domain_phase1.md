## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — Methodological rigor requires study design, data treatment, uncertainty reporting, and reproducibility sufficient to make the domain results credible and independently assessable. The methodology reviewer owns this dimension.

D2 — Domain accuracy requires technically correct terminology and results, faithful treatment of related research, and claims that accord with the available evidence in the field. As domain reviewer, I assess whether substantive factual or conceptual errors change the scientific interpretation.

D3 — Argumentative coherence requires the central proposition to remain consistent across the work and its conclusions to follow from the evidence without reasoning defects that compromise the argument. This is owned by the argumentation reviewer, with methodology also eligible.

D4 — Cross-disciplinary relevance requires definitions and implications understandable to readers in neighboring fields, with interdisciplinary assertions supported sufficiently for those readers to evaluate them. This belongs to the perspective reviewer.

D5 — Writing and structure require a clear organization and explanation, useful figures and tables, and conformity with applicable presentation conventions. The editor-in-chief owns this assessment.

D6 — Venue fit and contribution concern whether the work offers a sufficiently original and consequential contribution for its intended readership and configured destination. The editor-in-chief owns this dimension; no institutional or venue alignment can be inferred without supplied criteria.
