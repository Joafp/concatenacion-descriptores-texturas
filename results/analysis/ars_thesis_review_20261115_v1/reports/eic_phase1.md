## Contract Paraphrase

D1 — Editorial confidence in an empirical study depends on a defensible research design, careful treatment of data, transparent uncertainty reporting, and enough procedural detail for independent reproduction. The methodology reviewer owns this assessment.

D2 — The specialist account should use technical concepts accurately, represent earlier work fairly, and keep factual assertions consistent with the relevant evidence. The domain reviewer owns this assessment.

D3 — The central argument should remain consistent across the manuscript, and its conclusions should follow from the evidence without a logical gap that undermines the thesis. The designated argument and methodology reviewers assess this dimension.

D4 — Readers in neighboring fields should be able to follow the motivation, terminology, and implications, while any claims that extend across disciplines should have adequate support. The perspective reviewer owns this assessment.

D5 — Editorial assessment considers whether the manuscript's organization, prose, figures, and tables allow readers to understand the research and its findings. Conventions can be assessed only where an applicable basis is available.

D6 — Editorial assessment considers whether the work identifies a distinct contribution and explains its significance for its stated readership. Fit to an institutional rubric or publication target requires a supplied target; none is available here, so no such alignment or grading claim will be made.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure

dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A clear progression from problem and objectives through methods, findings, and conclusions; consistent terminology; readable prose; and figures and tables with sufficient labels, captions, and cross-references to support interpretation within the stated thesis scope.
what_triggers_block: Organization or exposition persistently prevents a reader from reconstructing the research question, procedure, or principal findings, requiring substantial structural rewriting.
what_triggers_warn: Local repetition, ambiguous passages, inconsistent labels, or incomplete captions impede reading but can be repaired without rebuilding the manuscript's overall structure.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution

dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit research gap, a clearly distinguished author contribution, and a bounded explanation of its value within the author-stated empirical thesis scope; treat unavailable institutional or journal requirements as unassessable rather than inferred standards.
what_triggers_block: The contribution remains materially indistinguishable from the described prior work or the thesis does not explain how its reported work addresses its stated research problem, requiring substantive reframing or support.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is identifiable but its distinction, practical meaning, or scope is presented too implicitly or inconsistently and needs focused clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript explicitly establishes that its purported original contribution is wholly prior work without any distinguishable author contribution, defeating the thesis's central contribution claim rather than merely weakening its explanation.
