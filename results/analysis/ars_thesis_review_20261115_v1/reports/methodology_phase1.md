## Contract Paraphrase

D1 concerns whether empirical design, data preparation and separation, uncertainty analysis, and documentation permit credible and repeatable assessment of the methods under study.

D2 concerns whether technical concepts, empirical statements, and descriptions of earlier research are accurate; methodological interpretation must rest on sound domain premises.

D3 concerns whether objectives, procedures, evidence, and conclusions form a consistent argument, with conclusions restricted to what the design can establish.

D4 concerns whether neighboring disciplines can understand the framing and definitions, and whether broader implications have adequate evidential support.

D5 concerns whether organization, explanations, and visual presentation make procedures and evidence inspectable, together with adherence to any explicitly supplied presentation requirements.

D6 concerns whether the contribution is meaningful for its intended scholarly audience and meets supplied target requirements; judging institutional or venue alignment requires an actual criteria source.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: Explicit research questions, fair comparator definitions, data provenance and independence, leakage controls, separation of selection from evaluation, reproducible preprocessing and tuning, appropriate uncertainty and comparison procedures, and traceability of results to methods.
what_triggers_block: A material design or analysis defect prevents a central reported comparison from supporting its stated inference and requires substantive reanalysis or additional validation.
what_triggers_warn: The evaluation is interpretable but reporting omissions or limited sensitivity and uncertainty analyses reduce confidence or reproducibility without establishing that the central comparison is invalid.
what_triggers_fatal: Direct evidence establishes that every central comparison is irreparably compromised within the presented study, such as systematic evaluation contamination leaving no usable independent evidence.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: A traceable chain from research objectives through operational definitions and experimental contrasts to conclusions, consistent interpretation of outcomes, consideration of alternative explanations, and explicit limits on causal and generalization claims.
what_triggers_block: A major inferential step is unsupported by experimental contrasts or contradicts reported evidence and requires substantial reconstruction of the conclusions or supporting analysis.
what_triggers_warn: Local overstatements, ambiguous scope, or insufficient discussion of alternative explanations weaken the argument while a defensible central conclusion remains recoverable through clarification.
what_triggers_fatal: The central thesis depends on a demonstrably false premise or logically incompatible claims such that its conclusion cannot be recovered from the presented evidence through bounded revision.
